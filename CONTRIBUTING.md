# Contributing to Martialis

Suggestions are very welcomed: additions, corrections, sources, links and discussion; this is done
through the Discussions interface, or Issues if it's not about the actual database content but
something tied with the model or tools.

Pull requests are not used, so do not open one.

## How to contribute

**Create or add to a Discussion**: follow the instructions there, but the short version is that
dicussion topics should have the ULID of the entity in the title, since that's how they get picked
up by the web application. All discussions are public, so consider that when contributing.

## What a good suggestion looks like

- **Every claim cites at least one source**, with a locator and a direct quotation where the wording
  matters.
- **Disagreeing sources are included**, not left out.

SUggestions can be made in any language that I can translate: this means several European languages
directly, but more importantly it means that it is not required that comments are made in English:
when discussing someone from Germany, it's perfectly acceptable to contribute in German, for
example. I will do my best to translate, which given today's technology is simple.

## Licensing

By submitting content for the database, in a Discussion, an issue, or the suggestion form on
martial.is, you dedicate it to the public domain under **CC0-1.0**, covering copyright and any sui generis database right you may hold, and you
represent that it is your own statement of fact with citations rather than material copied from a
copyrighted or database-rights-protected work. Short quotations given as evidence are not part of
that dedication: they stay with their rightsholders. The database itself stays CC-BY-SA-4.0. See
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md#inbound-licensing).

For a substantial donation (a whole researched lineage, scans, photographs), contact the maintainer
to arrange a short individual agreement.

Disagreements are settled through the debate in Discussions; when consensus fails, the project lead
decides and states the reasoning.
